## Before the course starts

- [ ] AI rules in the syllabus, and the relevant one in every assignment brief.
- [ ] The [[rule:s-scope|student policy]] linked in the course room on Athena.
- [ ] Five minutes in the first lecture to go through the rules and the *why*.
- [ ] Students told how to disclose, with an example.
- [ ] The in-class or oral parts of the assessment in the schedule (chapter 5).
- [ ] Same rules agreed with co-teachers and TAs.

## Three things to take with you

1. **Under our rules, unauthorised AI use is hard to spot.** You can't use detectors, and good AI text doesn't look like AI. Don't build your course on catching it.
2. **So examine the important part in the room.** In-class writing, in-class exams, oral checks, seminars where students explain their own work. Let take-home work be practice and preparation.
3. **Keep the SRC rules, make them concrete, and say them on day one.** Adapt them only if you know these tools well and have a reason.
