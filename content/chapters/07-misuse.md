## When you suspect misuse

<p class="lead">Coming in the next build.</p>

A branching case: a polished essay, and a student who can't explain its key concept in seminar. What you can do: talk to the student, ask them to explain, use an oral follow-up, document, refer. And the backstop: work that doesn't show the learning outcomes fails.
