## Why now

<p class="lead">Coming in the next build.</p>

This chapter opens with a real case from an SRC course (anonymised): students handing in polished work they couldn't explain in seminar. Then a short game, **Spot the AI**: four short student answers, you guess which were written by AI. The point it makes: you can't reliably tell, and neither can [[AI detectors]]. That's why the rules and the assessment design matter more than suspicion.
