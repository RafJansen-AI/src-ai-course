# About this course

An interactive course on generative AI for teaching staff at the Stockholm Resilience Centre. It's built around SRC's own AI policies, which take precedence over anything else, including this course. If the course and the policy ever disagree, the policy wins; please tell us.

**Last reviewed:** 12 November 2026. Content about what AI tools can do is reviewed each term.

**Your data:** everything you enter stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and there are no accounts or tracking. Clearing your browser data, or "Start over" in the chapter menu, removes it.

**How it was made:** written by Raf Jansen (SRC) with Claude (Anthropic), which drafted text, interactive parts and code. All content was reviewed and edited by SRC staff. Some ideas build on Eva Mårell-Olsson's presentation *AI in higher education* (2026) and on guidance from Umeå University's Department of Education.

**Contact:** Raf Jansen, AI consultant, Stockholm Resilience Centre.
